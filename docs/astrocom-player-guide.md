# AstroCom — Player Guide

- **Audience:** Players and Trusted Players
- **Module:** Kakeman89's Datacron
- **Attribution:** Kakeman89
- **Phase:** 6 AstroCom MVP

---

## What you can do

When AstroCom is enabled for the world:

1. Open AstroCom from the scene controls (if your role is Player or higher).
2. Choose Canon or Legends continuity.
3. Search planets by name or alias.
4. Apply filters (Region, Sector, System, Grid, Route, classifications) as available.
5. Open a result to read the **player-visible Journal**.

That is the full player workflow for the MVP.

---

## What you cannot do

Players do **not**:

- Rebuild packs or regenerate content
- View source / conflict / review / provenance tooling
- Approve enrichment or change authored data
- Toggle the AstroCom feature setting
- Fetch images or external web data through AstroCom

Packs are module content maintained offline by Kakeman89. Your world does not invent new planets at the table.

---

## Tips

- If AstroCom is missing from scene controls, the GM may have disabled the feature or your role may be Observer/Limited.
- Empty or unavailable results usually mean packs are not loaded or filters exclude everything — ask the GM; do not try to “fix” packs in-world.
- Journals follow normal Foundry visibility. You only open entries your permissions allow.
